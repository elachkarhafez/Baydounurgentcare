Hi My Name Is Noor, This Is Coffee Talks.

I’m Noor, a social media manager and content creator based in Metro Detroit, working with businesses across all industries and niches nationwide. Coffee Talks started with a love for coffee, cozy corners, and capturing the little moments that make life special.

Almost a decade later, that passion has become my full-time world, helping business owners share the heart behind what they do. Behind every brand is someone who took a chance on a dream, and getting to tell those stories is my favorite part of this work.

Whether you’ve trusted me with your business, shared my content, or simply followed along, thank you for being part of mine.
