Meet Noor.

Hi 🤍 I'm Noor, the heart behind Coffee Talks.

I'm a content creator and social media manager based in Dearborn, Michigan. This space started from a simple love for coffee, cozy corners and catching the sweet little moments that make life feel full. Almost a decade later, it's my full-time world.

I make content for all the pretty things, from coffee and food to spaces and the moments that deserve to be seen, and I manage social media for some of the most beautiful cafés, restaurants and small businesses in the city. Thank you for every message and every shared cup.
