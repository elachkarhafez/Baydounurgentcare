Chocolate Maya, ground in-house

Chocolate in Guatemala and Mexico goes back to the Maya and the Aztecs. We keep it simple and do it the old way.

  1. We grind fair-trade cacao beans right here.
  2. We blend it with our own mix of sugar and spices, nothing artificial and no preservatives.
  3. It goes into hot milk or water with cinnamon and gets whipped until it's rich and frothy.
