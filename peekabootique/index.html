    Good to know

    The questions moms ask us most.

    What is the difference between 3D and 4D/HD/5D?

    3D is a still image, while 4D/HD/5D adds motion, so it looks like a video. HD/5D uses software to change the focal points and lighting so you can see all the details of the baby.

    When is the best time to come in?

    It depends on what you want to see. Many moms come in twice: at 15 to 26 weeks to see the baby as a whole, and again at 25 to 34 weeks for a more detailed face. Between 28 and 32 weeks babies get more fat and a more defined face. After 34 weeks it gets harder as baby runs out of room, but we have gotten great images up to 39 weeks.

    How do I get the best pictures?

    Water. Drink plenty of it for at least 7 days before your appointment. About half an hour before, have a fruit juice so your baby is awake, unless your doctor has told you not to.

    Is it safe?

    3D and 4D/HD/5D ultrasound use the same frequency your doctor uses for a normal 2D ultrasound. According to the FDA, there have been no proven side effects from its proper use. We require that you are under prenatal care, and our technicians do not give medical advice or diagnoses.

    What if you cannot see the gender or get images?

    If you are over 15 weeks and the gender cannot be seen, or we cannot get 3D/4D/HD/5D images because of the baby's position or other factors, we will rescan you for free within 14 days.

    Can family and kids come?

    Yes. Bring your family and friends to share the moment. Children are welcome too, and becoming a big sister or brother is a big deal here.

    Do you take insurance?

    No. This is an elective ultrasound, so we take major credit and debit cards and cash.

    When do I get my pictures and videos?

    Right after your session.
