Why booza stretches

Booza gets its pull from mastic, a resin from the mastic tree, and sahlab, a flour made from orchid root. It's churned in a frozen metal drum and beaten the whole time with a long wooden pestle, so it ends up dense and chewy instead of airy.

  1. 1Milk, sugar, sahlab and ground mastic go into the cold drum.
  2. 2We pound and fold it by hand until it pulls off the pestle in long ropes.
  3. 3Every scoop gets rolled in crushed pistachio right before it's handed over.
