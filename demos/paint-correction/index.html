What we do

Most cars need some polishing before they get coated. We'll look at yours under the lights and tell you what it actually needs.

01

Paint correction

Machine polishing that takes out swirls, light scratches and haze. We measure paint depth first so we only take off what we need to.

02

Ceramic coating

A hard, slick layer over the clear coat. Water beads up and rolls off, and washing takes about half the time.

03

Wheels and interior

Faces and barrels cleaned, brake dust gone, cabin vacuumed and wiped down. Good before a coating or on its own.
