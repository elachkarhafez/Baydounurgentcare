    How it's made

    Brined, breaded, pressed, poured.

    The scroll story: the syrup runs down the steps as you go.

    The night before

    Buttermilk brine

    Chicken goes into seasoned buttermilk overnight, so it's juicy all the way through.

    To order

    Hand-breaded

    Dredged, dipped and dredged again when the ticket comes in, so the crust has all those craggy bits.

    Hot oil

    Fried golden

    Into the fryer until it's deep gold and crackly, then a rest on the rack while the waffle catches up.

    Same minute

    Waffle pressed

    Batter into a hot iron. Crisp outside, soft inside, and 36 pockets ready for the good part.
