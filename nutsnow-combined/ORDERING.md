# Nuts Now website: how it works

A multi-page shop for Nuts Now (Allen Park and Dearborn Heights), in English at `/` and Arabic at `/ar/`. The pages are plain HTML generated by `tools/build.mjs` and committed, so Vercel just serves the files. Two small Vercel functions do the server checks.

## What's on the site

- **Home**: the roaster video hero with the roast-line belts, "Start your order" / "Find a store", the Google rating, both shops with open/closed status, aisles, viral picks, the Dubai chocolate aisle, how ordering works, Google review quotes and the visit section.
- **Shop** (`/shop/`), **10 aisles** (`/shop/<aisle>/`) and **collections** (Dubai chocolate, Swedish candy, sugar free). Aisle pages filter by sub-aisle (kept in the URL as `?type=`) and sort by price or name.
- **792 product pages** (`/product/<slug>/`). Items with a verified shelf price can be ordered (by ¼ lb or by the piece). The rest say "Price in store" and can't be added.
- **Search** over every product, from the header or by pressing `/`. On the Arabic site it also matches Arabic aisle names (for example كاجو).
- **Bag** (drawer, plus a bar at the bottom on phones). Items sold by weight can be ticked "In my mix bag" to be weighed into one bag.
- **Pickup shop picker**: "Pickup at …" in the top bar, also in the bag. The choice is remembered (`localStorage` key `nn-store`) and preselected at checkout.
- **Language switch**: "عربي" / "English" in the top bar, the menu and the footer. It goes to the same page in the other language. Product names stay as on the shelf; everything else is translated, and the Arabic pages are right-to-left.
- **Checkout** (`/checkout/`):
  1. Pickup or delivery.
  2. Which shop.
  3. A pickup time (ASAP or a slot today/tomorrow, within the store's hours, prep time and last-pickup cutoff), or for delivery, the address and live courier prices.
  4. Your details.
  The server re-checks everything, then the order goes to the shop by phone, Instagram or copy-paste. It says **"Not paid yet"**: online payment isn't switched on.
- **Stores** (`/stores/`): hours, maps, directions, call buttons, Google rating per shop, and DoorDash / Uber Eats buttons once their store links exist.
- **About** (`/about/`), with a contact form. Until the shop gives an email (`settings.contactEmail` in the catalog), it copies the message and points to the call and Instagram buttons; with an email set, it opens the customer's email app. **Photo credits** (`/credits/`) lists every product photo's author and license.

## Aisle transition

Tapping any aisle or the Shop link drops a mix of nuts and candy over the screen around the logo. Once the screen is covered, the next page opens and the pile falls away (`js/transition.js`, about 0.8 s each way). It is skipped for people who turn off motion, and for links opened in a new tab.

- **The pieces** are real 3D models rendered once into `img/tx/pieces.webp` (48 sprites):
  - nuts: cashews, almonds, pistachios, hazelnuts, walnuts, pecans
  - candy: gummy bears, sour worms, peach rings, Swedish fish, licorice, truffles, candy-coated chocolates, yogurt pretzels, dried mango
- **To re-render** after changing `src/pieces/scene.js`, run from the repo root:
  ```bash
  bash .claude/skills/ela-studio/kit/build3d/build.sh nutsnow-combined/src/pieces/scene.js nutsnow-combined/src/pieces/bundle.js
  python3 -m http.server 8765 &
  node nutsnow-combined/src/pieces/render.mjs /tmp/pieces
  ```
  Then pack the PNGs eight across at 160 px into `img/tx/pieces.webp`, in file order.

## Type and layout

- **Fonts**: Archivo everywhere (condensed for headings and prices, italic for the tagline), with Cairo for Arabic.
- **Sizes** follow the ELA formatting standards:
  - hero headline 53–56px on laptops and desktops, 30–34px on phones
  - section headings 38–40px / 24px
  - body text 15–17px
  - container 1200px
- **Tested** at 1366×768, 1440×900, 1920×1080, 390×844 and 768×1024, plus 200% zoom and a 20px browser font. Nothing overflows sideways, and the hero headline, text and buttons fit on a 1366×768 laptop without scrolling.

## Delivery: DoorDash and Uber

Delivery prices come live from **DoorDash Drive** and **Uber Direct** through `api/delivery.js`. Both are courier services that deliver from your own shop, booked from your own website. They need the shop's own business accounts, and the keys go only into the Vercel project's **Environment Variables** (never into the code):

| Provider | Sign up | Variables |
|---|---|---|
| DoorDash Drive | developer.doordash.com, create a Drive app, then a production access key | `DOORDASH_DEVELOPER_ID`, `DOORDASH_KEY_ID`, `DOORDASH_SIGNING_SECRET` |
| Uber Direct | direct.uber.com / developer.uber.com, Direct API credentials | `UBER_CLIENT_ID`, `UBER_CLIENT_SECRET`, `UBER_CUSTOMER_ID` |

- **Without keys**, picking delivery says it's being set up and suggests pickup. Either provider works alone.
- **With keys**, customers get both prices (cheapest first) with the arrival time. The order sent to the shop includes the courier, the quoted fee and its reference.
- **Booking stays with the shop**: no courier is booked and nothing is charged automatically. The shop books the courier when it confirms the order. When online payment is added, that step can call each provider's accept/create endpoint.
- **Marketplace listings**: if the shops get listed on the DoorDash or Uber Eats apps, put the store page links in `data/catalog.json` under `settings.delivery.doordash.storeUrls` / `settings.delivery.ubereats.storeUrls` (`ap` / `dh`) and rebuild. The buttons then appear on the store cards.

## Updating products and prices

1. Edit the owner's spreadsheet, then run `python3 tools/import_catalog.py <catalog.xlsx>`. This writes `data/catalog.json`: products, prices, units, aisles, collections and featured items. The spreadsheet itself is internal and isn't committed.
2. Product photos are matched by `tools/image_rules.py` (product name → photo key). Each key is `img/p/<key>.webp` (640×640) with `img/p/credits/<key>.json`. Photos must be real photographs under the Unsplash License (free photos only), CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA. No AI images and no shop or competitor photos.
3. Run `node tools/build.mjs` to regenerate every English and Arabic page and the sitemap.
4. Interface text for both languages is in `tools/i18n.mjs` (pages) and the `CLIENT_T` block in `tools/build.mjs` (text the browser scripts show).

## Rules the server enforces

`js/order-core.js` is shared by the browser and the server:
- **Prices**: integer cents, ¼ lb steps, lines rounded half-up. The server never trusts the browser's total and reports when prices changed.
- **Pickup times**: worked out in Detroit time, with prep time, opening hours, last pickup and closures.
- **Delivery**: only while the shop can still get an order ready today.
- **Unpriced items**: can't be ordered.
- **Each-items**: up to 40 per line.

## Files

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `data/catalog.json` | Products, prices, aisles, stores, hours, pickup and delivery settings (generated). |
| `data/reviews.json` | Google rating, review counts and quotes (verified). |
| `tools/build.mjs`, `tools/i18n.mjs` | Page generator and translations. |
| `tools/import_catalog.py`, `tools/image_rules.py` | Spreadsheet import and photo matching. |
| `js/order-core.js` | Pricing, amounts, pickup and delivery rules (browser + server). |
| `js/site.js`, `js/home.js`, `js/checkout.js` | Bag, search, shop picker and language strings; home hero; checkout. |
| `api/quote.js` | `POST /api/quote`: re-prices the bag and re-checks pickup or delivery. |
| `api/delivery.js` | `GET` which couriers are on; `POST` live DoorDash/Uber prices. |
| `tests/` | Unit tests, browser tests and the local server. |

## Running the tests

```bash
node --test tests/core.test.js
node tests/serve.js 8790                                                    # browse at http://127.0.0.1:8790
NN_FAKE_COURIERS=1 node tests/serve.js 8791 "" 2026-10-14T20:50:00Z         # fixed store clock + pretend couriers
node tests/e2e.mjs /tmp 8791                                                # desktop + phone journeys, English + Arabic
```
`NN_FAKE_COURIERS=1` answers the DoorDash and Uber APIs locally. It checks the DoorDash JWT signature and the request fields, so the delivery flow can be tested without real keys.

## Re-rendering the roaster video

The scene is `src/roaster/scene.js` (three.js). From the repo root:

```bash
bash .claude/skills/ela-studio/kit/build3d/build.sh nutsnow-combined/src/roaster/scene.js nutsnow-combined/src/roaster/bundle.js
python3 -m http.server 8765 &                          # serve the repo root
node nutsnow-combined/src/roaster/render.mjs /tmp/rf   # 390 frames (12 s + 1 s tail, 30 fps), 3 passes each
```
Each frame is saved as three passes: `f` (the machine on black), `m` (its white silhouette) and `s` (the floor shadow). Then:

1. Composite each frame onto white: `out = f + 255·(1 − m)·(1 − 0.42·blur(s))`, with the shadow fading out 140 px from the frame edges.
2. Blend frames 360–389 into 0–29 so the loop has no jump. The drum turn repeats exactly every 12 s, and the one shake (at 6.2 s) is far from the seam; only the nuts and flames need the blend.
3. Crop to (120, 150, 1080, 1290) and scale to 768×912.
4. Encode `roaster.mp4` (H.264, `+faststart`) and `roaster.webm` (VP9). Frame 0 becomes the poster.
5. Merge every frame's silhouette, grow it 1 px, and save it as `roaster-mask.webp` with alpha.
