Our story

Fresh roasted traditions

Before the shops, there was the roastery. The family behind Nuts Now has run a nut and seed roastery in Allen Park for more than 15 years. Flavoured and roasted cashews are still our number one seller, and we source nuts from all over the world.

Today Nuts Now is two shops, in Allen Park and Dearborn Heights, with walls of nuts, seeds, dried fruit, chocolate, Swedish candy, Turkish delight and spices, all sold by the pound so you can take as little or as much as you like.

Come in and ask for a sample. Or order here and we'll have it weighed and ready.
