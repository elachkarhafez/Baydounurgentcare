Our story

From a bank on the river to a kitchen on the river bank

  1. Then

    Talmer Bank & Trust

    301 Summer Street was a bank, on St. Clair River Drive next to the fire department with Waterfront Park across the road.

  2. The plan

    Food, drinks, sports and the river

    A new spot downtown, a short walk from the Walpole–Algonac ferry.

  3. Now

    Building it out

    The work is underway. More updates, sneak peeks and the opening date are on the way.

  4. Next

    Opening day

    Not announced yet. You'll hear it on Instagram first.

Built by people from here

Owner Scott Krall is from the area and has wanted to open his own restaurant in Algonac for a long time. The project is backed by local investors who believe in Algonac and the communities around it.

"We're just getting started, and there's a lot more to come."
