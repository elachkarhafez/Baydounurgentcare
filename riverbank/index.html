About

The old bank on Summer Street

The River Bank Kitchen & Bar is opening in the former Talmer Bank & Trust building in downtown Algonac. Waterfront Park and the St. Clair River are right across the road, and the Walpole Island ferry is a short walk away.

The kitchen will serve pizza, burgers, steaks and salads, with drinks from the bar. The opening date and the full menu go up on Instagram first.
