Henrietta Street

A Birmingham staple, inspired by the pizzerias of Brooklyn.

We've been firing pies on Henrietta Street since 1996. Big, foldable New York slices with a crisp, blistered crust from the open flame, and the same short list of real ingredients on every one.

  • San Marzano tomatoesCertified organic, for a bright, simple sauce
  • Imported Italian cheesePlus fresh mozzarella, fontina and Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • Italian olive oilExtra virgin only, never seed oils
  • Italian flourNo fillers, additives or bleaching

The menu

Every pizza comes in 10" or 16", and slices are out all day. Any pie can be made with a gluten-free vegan cauliflower crust (10") or vegan cheese.

Something for everyone at the table

Halal meats

Gluten-free & vegan

Every pizza on the menu can be made on our cauliflower crust with vegan cheese.
