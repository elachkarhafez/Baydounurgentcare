The space

A pre-fab building, hacked into a garden.

A 5,778-square-foot metal building in Core City, opened up to light and greenery, with a street-facing plaza, garden-side seating and a pergola that carries the brewery out into the garden. These are the design renderings.

Architecture: END Studio · Landscape: JIMA Studio · Branding: @mondubray · Development: Jumi Develops
