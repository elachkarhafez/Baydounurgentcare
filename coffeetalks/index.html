    Meet Noor,The Heart Behind It.

    Hi 🤍 I’m Noor, the heart behind Coffee Talks. I’m a content creator and social media manager based in Dearborn, Michigan.

    This space began from a simple love: coffee, cozy corners and capturing the sweet little moments that make life feel full. Almost a decade later, that passion has grown into my full-time world. I now create content for all the pretty things, from coffee and food to spaces and the moments that deserve to be seen.

    I also have the privilege of managing social media for some of the most beautiful cafés, restaurants and small businesses in the city, which truly feels like the greatest blessing. I’m endlessly grateful for every connection, every message and every shared cup. Here’s to many more stories, sips and memories together.
