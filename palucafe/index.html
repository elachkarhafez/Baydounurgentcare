Why “Palu”

A town with deep roots.

Palu is a historic town on the old Silk Road with a long Armenian heritage. It's where May Kafafian's family comes from.

In her memory

Named for May.

Owner Khachig Kafafian opened the café in honor of his late wife May, a beloved member of the Armenian community who held on to its traditions.

The idea

A home away from home.

Palu Café is envisioned as a gathering place, a home away from home for all Armenians, and a welcoming space for anyone interested in experiencing Armenian culture.
