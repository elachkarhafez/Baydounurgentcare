01 — How it works

How a cut goes

Every appointment starts with a quick talk about length and how you wear it. Then the sides are tapered and blended, the edges are lined up, and the top is shaped to match.

  1. I

    Consult

    Length, fade height and how you style it day to day.

  2. II

    Taper & blend

    Sides and back taken down and blended into the top.

  3. III

    Line-up & style

    Edges cleaned up, then the top styled: curls, slick back or textured.
