    Started on a truck

    A Detroit original.

    The truck

    Chicken & waffles on wheels

    It started as a butter-yellow food truck with syrup painted dripping down the sides, one of Michigan's first halal food trucks. Waffles, tenders, syrup. That's the whole idea.

    2018

    Belle Isle

    The truck parks on Belle Isle and becomes the island's chicken spot.

    2025

    A building on the island

    They move into Belle Isle's old concession stand. The internet decided it was a restaurant in a bathroom. It isn't, it just shares an awning. So they made a video about it and kept frying.

    Now

    Dearborn & Southgate

    A counter at The Canteen at Midtown in Dearborn, a spot on Dix-Toledo in Southgate, and beef tallow in every fryer.
