The principles we work by

Every member of our staff follows them, on every visit.

  1. Meet the unique health needs of each patient and family.
  2. Help each person reach their best level of function.
  3. Respect every client's dignity and worth.
  4. Give care in the least restricted setting: at home.
  5. Involve patients and families in planning their care.
  6. Never discriminate by age, race, color, religion, national origin or disability.
