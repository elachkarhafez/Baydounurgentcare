Carbon vs ceramic

High noon in July.

Your car’s been parked in the sun since nine. Here’s what each film actually does about it.

01 · Glare & UV

Both kill the glare.

Carbon and ceramic both block nearly all of the sun’s UV and take the squint out of driving.

02 · Heat

Ceramic kills the heat.

Ceramic stops far more of the sun’s infrared heat, so the cabin stays cooler and the AC works less.

03 · Signal

Neither kills your signal.

No metal in either film, so your phone, GPS and radio work like normal.
