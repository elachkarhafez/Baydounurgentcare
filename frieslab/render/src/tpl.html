From food videos to a kitchen

Ahmad Wehbe spent four years making food content before he opened Fries Lab. The fries came first, messy and loaded, and the burgers followed.

In November 2024 our shop in Dahye was destroyed in the airstrikes. We kept cooking from a cloud kitchen in Beirut, came back to Haret Hreik, and now we're in Antelias too.
