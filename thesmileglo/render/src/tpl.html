    Good questions

    Is teeth whitening safe?

    When it’s done properly, yes. As a Registered Dental Hygienist, I check your teeth and gums first, protect your gums during whitening, and let you know if you’re ready.

    Does whitening hurt?

    It shouldn’t. Some people feel temporary sensitivity during or after whitening. I adjust as I go and can recommend products that keep you comfortable.

    How long do results last?

    It depends on your habits. Coffee, tea, red wine and tobacco all stain over time. With good care, results usually last a long while.

    What should I avoid after?

    For the first 24–48 hours, skip coffee, tea, red wine, soda, dark juices, berries, tomato sauce and soy sauce, and no smoking or vaping. If it would stain a white shirt, it can stain your teeth. Water, milk, chicken, white rice and plain yogurt are all safe.

    Why see a hygienist instead of using strips?

    Professional-grade whitening goes further and more evenly, your gums are protected, and I’m watching for sensitivity the whole time. You also get a professional opinion on what’s realistic for your teeth.
