It started with Wazira's desserts

For years Wazira Alrabeei baked for family and friends. In August 2023 she and her brother Ebrahim opened Socotra on Packard St, and about three dozen of us from the extended family pitched in to get it going.

Our qahwa comes from Ibb in Yemen. The way we spice and brew it hasn't changed in over 500 years, and it tastes earthy and a little chocolatey, with a hint of dried fruit. The dragon's blood tree in our logo is from Socotra, and it's on the mural inside too.
